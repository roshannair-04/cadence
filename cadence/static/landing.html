Open Cadence See how it works

Free. No account. Your files stay in a private workspace tied to your browser.

From a folder of slides to a plan for tonight

  1. 1

    Add your material

    Upload PDFs, PowerPoints or text files. Cadence splits them into sections, finds the facts and key terms, and keeps your own wording. Nothing is made up.

  2. 2

    Read in layers

    Start with three key points, read the full notes, go deeper with self-explanation prompts, or ask for the simpler version. After wrong answers, Cadence offers it before you ask.

  3. 3

    Get quizzed on what matters

    Every answer updates what Cadence thinks you know. The next question targets weak or fading sections, and it tells you why it picked it.

A small model of your memory

Two well-known ideas, kept simple and visible.

Bayesian Knowledge Tracing
Each section starts at a modest guess of what you know. A right answer raises it, a wrong one lowers it, and lucky guesses are discounted.
A forgetting curve
Recall fades over time. When the estimate for a section drops below 70%, it comes back as due for review.
Nothing hidden
The dashboard shows the map, the curve and the reasons. It is an estimate, and it says so.

Made to be usable by everyone

  • Read aloud and keyboard first

    Answer whole quizzes without a mouse. Every chart has a table view.

  • Your reading comfort

    Atkinson Hyperlegible or Lexend, text size and spacing, higher contrast, reduced motion, light and dark.

  • Private by default

    Each browser gets its own workspace. Other people and other devices never see your files.

  • A way to check it works

    A pre-check and post-check show your learning gain, and a short survey scores the tool itself.
